# Changelog

## v1.0 (2026-10-14)

First release.
- **Options > GM Icon** opens a picker: 10 bundled icons on top, your own images (tokens, portraits, images, asset modules) below.
- The choice applies at once; OK keeps it, Cancel puts back the old one, FG Default returns to Fantasy Grounds' own icon.
- Saved per computer, so it follows the GM into every campaign and ruleset.
- Players see the same picture next to everything the GM says: chat, whispers, initiative rolls and Syrinscape links.
